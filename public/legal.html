3. Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliance)

Data Collection & Purpose

To provide our services, we collect strictly necessary data: your email address (for account creation and security), your timezone (for release reminders), and your library data (tracked shows, ratings, custom links). This data is used solely to maintain your account, sync your library across devices, and push notifications.

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is securely stored on Supabase servers located in Ireland. We retain this data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently erased.

User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time. A direct "Delete my account" (Irreversible action) button is available in your profile settings for full control.

Cookies

Akasha uses only strictly necessary technical cookies (Local Storage) required to maintain a secure session via Supabase and prevent bots via hCaptcha. No advertising trackers or behavioral analytics tools are used.
